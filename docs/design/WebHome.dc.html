WHAT IT DOES

Live view and recordings

Watch the road, take screenshots, and download loop clips, photos and incidents.

Plate recognition

Finds plates in live view and saved clips, with a searchable history of every sighting.

Upscaling

Enhance a single frame, or export a clip in 1440p or 4K.

Footage in your browser

Connect S3-compatible or WebDAV storage, then watch your recordings on this website.
